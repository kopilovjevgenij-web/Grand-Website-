About Us

One Address for Every Occasion

GRAND is a restaurant and bar for family lunches and evening get-togethers, banquet halls for celebrations of any size, a night program with karaoke and DJs every Friday and Saturday, and a cosy mini-hotel for those who want to stay a little longer.

  • Restaurant, bar and terrace — breakfast, set lunches, dinner
  • Banquet, Pink and Conference halls for events
  • Karaoke and disco every Friday and Saturday until 05:00
  • A mini-hotel with rooms and suites right at the restaurant
  • Summer terrace and a trampoline — for the whole family
